| Question Answering | Provide accurate answers to natural language questions | OpenAI GPT, Claude, Google Gemini, IBM Watson |
| Text Classification | Categorize text into predefined labels or topics | AWS Comprehend, Google AutoML, Azure, Hugging Face |
| Paraphrasing & Rewriting | Rephrase content while preserving meaning | OpenAI GPT, Claude, QuillBot, Grammarly |
